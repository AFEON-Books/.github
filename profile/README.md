# Welcome to AFEON Books 📚

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **AFEON Books**! We are a creative, student-led publishing initiative dedicated to crafting, sharing, and selling captivating stories. Originally started by 7th-grade students at Alvarado, our mission is to deliver engaging fiction while supporting our local community and school programs. 

This organization profile serves as the central hub for our digital infrastructure, including our main platform repository, web services, and open-source tools.

---

## 🚀 Our Core Repository: afeonbooks

The heartbeat of our digital presence is [**afeon-books/afeonbooks**](https://github.com/afeon-books/afeonbooks). This repository houses the main application and source code for our official web platform, where readers can preview our work, purchase books, and buy custom merchandise.

### Key Features of the Platform
* **Interactive Previews:** Seamless digital book previews to give readers a glimpse into our stories before buying.
* **E-Commerce Suite:** An optimized online store supporting both physical book sales (complete with limited-edition stickers) and instant digital eBook deliveries.
* **Community Fundraising Integrations:** Automated or structured revenue distribution built to ensure sales support local educational groups like the Alvarado PTSA and Shelyn PTA.

## 📖 Current Publications & Projects

Our current focus features our headline mystery and adventure series, which showcases creative storytelling combined with tech-forward distribution:

* **The Terror Of The Evil Capybara (Book 1):** Follow the legendary detectives White Dragon, Evanzilla, Naomi Da Apple, and Kevin as they face off against a furry, glasses-wearing capybara mastermind. 
* **The Rise Of A New Enemy (Book 2):** The stakes get higher, the action expands, and our detectives push their limits to defeat new threats.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

While individual configurations can be found inside the `afeonbooks` repository, our organization prioritizes clean, lightweight web technology to maintain a fast and accessible reading environment:

* **Frontend:** Modern HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript optimized for responsive mobile and desktop viewing.
* **E-Commerce & Backend:** Secure transaction handling for seamless processing of physical books, stickers, and digital downloads.
* **Hosting & Automation:** Integrated workflows designed for reliable uptime and painless deployment pipelines.
